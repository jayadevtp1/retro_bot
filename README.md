
# Desktop Bot

Desktop Bot is a mini AI companion robot built to sit right on my desk and bring a bit of personality to my workspace. Inspired by the M5Stack Chan AI bot and the nostalgic charm of vintage CRT monitors, I wanted to create a compact, expressive desk buddy that can react, chat
and emotions on a crisp 2-inch LCD screen.

# Features

• Animated Facial Expressions: Displays expressive eyes and emotions on a crisp 2-inch LCD screen.

• Built-in Voice Interaction: Listens and talks back naturally using an onboard digital mic and speaker amplifier.

• Desk Utilities: Doubles as a handy desk gadget with a digital clock and ambient temperature display.

• Capacitive Touch Interaction: Responds to gentle taps and head pats with playful expressions and reactions.
